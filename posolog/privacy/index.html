Posolog

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how Posolog, the medication tracker for Android phones, tablets and Wear OS watches made by ychk (“we”, “us”), handles your information.

In short: your information is on your devices and, if you connect Google Drive, in your own Google Drive. We have no servers, we never receive your information, and we never sell or share it. Posolog has no ads and no analytics.

Information Posolog handles

Posolog handles only the information you choose to enter:

  • your medications and their schedules;
  • the doses you take;
  • the stock left in each box;
  • your prescriptions;
  • your health log;
  • your appointments.

Posolog uses this information only to provide its features to you: reminders, your dose history, stock tracking, exports and sync between your devices.

Where your information is kept

On your devices. Posolog stores your information on each phone, tablet and watch where you use it.

In your own Google Drive, if you connect it. To keep your devices up to date with each other, Posolog saves sync files in a folder named “Posolog” that it creates in your own Google Drive. These files are part of your Google account and are covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Nowhere else. Posolog has no servers. Your information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Access to your Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Posolog asks only for Google’s most limited Drive permission, which Google describes as “See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app”. With it, Posolog can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.

Posolog uses this access only to save and read its own sync files, so that your devices share the same information.

Posolog’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Encryption with a passphrase

You can choose to encrypt your sync files with a passphrase. With encryption on, the files in your Google Drive can be read only by Posolog on devices where you have entered your passphrase. We never see your passphrase and cannot recover it, so keep it somewhere safe.

What we don’t do

  • No servers: we never receive, store or see your information.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No third parties: Posolog shares your information with no one. Your sync files go only to your own Google Drive.
  • No selling or sharing: we never sell, rent or share your information.

Exports

When you export your information, Posolog creates the file and you choose where to save or send it. Posolog does not send exports anywhere on its own.

Deleting your information

You can delete everything at any time:

  1. On your devices: uninstall Posolog from each phone, tablet and watch. This deletes everything Posolog stored on that device.
  2. In Google Drive: delete the “Posolog” folder from your Google Drive. Deleted files go to your Drive trash, which Google empties after 30 days; empty it yourself to remove them right away.
  3. Posolog’s access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/connections (or Google Account > Security > Third-party connections), choose Posolog and remove its access.

We hold no copy of your information, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Your information is protected by the security of your devices and of your Google account. Passphrase encryption adds another layer of protection to your sync files.

Children

Posolog is not directed at children under 13. A parent or carer may use Posolog to manage a child’s medications; that information is handled exactly as described in this policy, on your devices and in your own Google Drive.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Posolog? Contact us on our support page on GitHub. Messages there are public, so please don’t include personal or health information.
