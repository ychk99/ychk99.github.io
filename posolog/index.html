Posolog

Your medications, organised.

Posolog is a medication tracker for Android. It reminds you when a dose is due, records what you have taken, tracks how much is left in each box and keeps your prescriptions, health log and appointments in one place.

  • Tablet
  • Wear OS watch

Everything in one place

  • Reminders

    A reminder for every dose, at the time you choose.

  • Dose log

    Mark each dose as taken and look back at your history.

  • Stock per box

    See how much is left in each box and know when to restock.

  • Prescriptions

    Keep your French prescriptions (ordonnances) with your treatments.

  • Health log

    Write down how you feel and keep a day-by-day record of your health.

  • Appointments

    Keep track of your upcoming medical appointments.

  • Exports

    Export your information whenever you need it, for yourself or your doctor.

  • Sync

    Your phone, tablet and watch share the same up-to-date information.

  • Watch app

    Your doses at a glance on your Wear OS watch.

On your phone, tablet and watch

Posolog works on Android phones and tablets, with a companion app for Wear OS watches. A change you make on one device shows up on your other devices.

Synced through your own Google Drive

Sync uses the Google account you already have. Posolog saves its sync files in a folder named “Posolog” in your own Google Drive, and your devices read them from there. Posolog can see only the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive. For extra protection, you can encrypt your sync files with a passphrase that only you know.

Privacy in brief

  • Your information is on your devices and in your own Google Drive, nowhere else.
  • No servers: your information is never sent to us.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No account to create, and nothing is ever sold or shared.
